Our Story

Founded by Mr. Muhammad Ismail, Jo Jo Electronic has been serving the Raiwind, Lahore community with premium home appliances for over a decade. What started as a small electronics repair shop has grown into a trusted destination for quality air conditioners, washing machines, fans, and modern kitchen appliances.

Our mission is simple: Quality Products, Honest Prices, Exceptional Service. We believe in building lasting relationships with our customers by offering genuine products from trusted brands like EcoStar, GFC, and Sonex.

Every product in our store is handpicked to ensure it meets our high standards of quality and reliability. When you shop at Jo Jo Electronic, you're not just buying an appliance — you're investing in peace of mind.
